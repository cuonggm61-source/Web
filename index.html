THE PHOTO

I love photo

I built this little corner of the web to share the photographs I collect — moments lifted from ordinary days and reworked by light, angle, and patience. Some images began as quiet observations on a morning walk, others as accidental collisions of timing and chance; each was edited, held, and sometimes reimagined until it felt ready to speak. Here you’ll find landscapes and fragments, portraits and details, fleeting gestures and still arrangements — a mixture of the intimate and the incidental, the planned and the spontaneous. My hope is that these pictures might offer you a small jolt of feeling, a memory unlocked, or a fresh way to look at the world; that in visiting, you might find something that lingers. Browse, linger, and come back when you want to see the ordinary made strange again.
